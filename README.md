# StreetRights

> **Know the rules. Understand your rights. Protect your livelihood.**

## 📖 About the Project

**StreetRights** is a mobile-first digital platform designed to help street vendors better understand the rules, permits, and processes related to their trading activities.

Street vendors play an important role in the economy and support the livelihoods of many individuals and families. However, vendors often face challenges when trying to understand complex municipal bylaws, permit requirements, trading regulations, fines, and processes relating to impounded goods.

StreetRights aims to bridge this information gap by using technology to make relevant information easier to access, understand, and navigate.

---

## 💡 The Problem

Street vendors may experience challenges such as:

- Difficulty understanding municipal bylaws and regulations.
- Limited access to clear information about trading permits.
- Complex permit application processes.
- Uncertainty about where they are legally allowed to trade.
- Difficulty understanding fines, notices, and official documents.
- Goods being impounded or confiscated.
- Limited knowledge of official processes when an incident occurs.
- Important information being written in complex or technical language.

These challenges can negatively affect vendors' ability to operate their businesses and support their livelihoods.

---

## 🎯 Our Solution

StreetRights provides a simple and accessible platform that helps street vendors navigate important information relating to their trading activities.

The platform simplifies complex information into practical, easy-to-understand guidance.

StreetRights is **not a replacement for legal professionals or municipal authorities**. Instead, it aims to help users better understand official information and navigate the appropriate processes and resources.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🗣️ Ask the Rules

Users can ask practical questions in simple language, such as:

- Do I need a permit to trade here?
- What documents do I need to apply for a permit?
- Where can I trade?
- What should I do if my goods are impounded?

The platform provides simplified guidance and directs users towards relevant official information.

### 📄 Permit Navigator

The Permit Navigator helps users understand the process of applying for a trading permit.

Users can:

- Select their municipality or trading area.
- Identify their type of trading activity.
- View relevant permit requirements.
- Follow a step-by-step process.
- Access official information and resources.

### 🚨 Incident Vault

The Incident Vault allows vendors to record important information when an incident occurs.

Users can capture:

- Date and time.
- Location.
- Description of the incident.
- Type of goods involved.
- Reference or receipt numbers.
- Supporting notes or evidence.

### 🤖 Explain This for Me

This feature uses AI to help simplify complex municipal notices, bylaws, or official documents.

The goal is to help users understand:

1. What the document is saying.
2. What it may mean for their trading activity.
3. What practical next steps or official resources may be relevant.

## 🧑🏾‍💼 User Journey

### Meet Thandi

Thandi is a street vendor who depends on her business to support herself and her family.

She wants to trade in a particular area but is unsure about the rules and whether she requires a permit.

Instead of searching through complicated documents, Thandi opens **StreetRights**.

### Step 1: Ask the Rules

Thandi selects her municipality and asks:

> "Do I need a permit to trade here?"

StreetRights provides simplified guidance and directs her towards relevant official information.

### Step 2: Navigate the Permit Process

Thandi uses the **Permit Navigator** to understand the requirements and steps involved in applying for permission to trade.

### Step 3: Record an Incident

If an enforcement incident occurs, such as goods being impounded, Thandi can use the **Incident Vault** to record important details.

### Step 4: Understand a Notice

If Thandi receives an official notice that she does not understand, she can use **Explain This for Me** to receive a simplified explanation.
